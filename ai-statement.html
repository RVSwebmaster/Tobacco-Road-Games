Generative AI Statement

Tobacco Road Games and Generative AI

Tobacco Road Games is an author-led, human-directed publishing imprint. Every Tobacco Road Games product begins with the ideas, design goals, table experience, creative judgment, and final approval of RV Sawyer.

Generative AI tools may be used as part of the production workflow. This may include brainstorming support, drafting assistance, editing passes, rules review, continuity checks, layout preparation, product description development, visual exploration, and artwork generation. These tools are used under human direction. They do not originate the product, determine its creative purpose, or replace the author.

The final responsibility for a Tobacco Road Games product rests with RV Sawyer. Concepts, mechanics, tone, structure, product identity, rules judgment, revisions, and publication approval remain human decisions.

Artwork is an important part of roleplaying game publishing, and small-press publishing requires practical production choices. When generative AI artwork is used in a finished Tobacco Road Games product, it is used as directed art, selected and approved for the needs of that specific product. Tobacco Road Games will disclose and tag AI-generated content according to the requirements of the marketplace where the product is sold.

Tobacco Road Games does not claim that products are “human-made without AI” when generative AI has contributed to the finished product. Likewise, Tobacco Road Games will not sell standalone AI-generated art packs, token packs, map packs, handout packs, or VTT asset packs in violation of marketplace policy.

The goal is simple: make useful, imaginative, affordable roleplaying game material with a clear human voice and responsible disclosure. AI may help carry tools to the table. It does not sit at the head of it.

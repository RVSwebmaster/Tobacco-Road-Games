The creative commitment

Every Book Must Have a Soul

Tobacco Road Games doesn’t publish material simply because it can be produced.

Every rule must express an idea. Every mechanic must belong to the subject. Every paragraph must earn its place.

We research before we borrow. We question easy answers. We revise what feels hollow, test what remains uncertain, and refuse to mistake volume for value.

Our work may draw upon history, folklore, theatre, religion, genre, and lived human experience. Those subjects deserve more than decoration. They deserve curiosity, care, and respect.

We use every tool available to make better games, but no tool replaces judgment, responsibility, or authorship. Nothing leaves Tobacco Road Games merely because it is finished.

It leaves when it has heart, mind, and teeth.

That is what we owe the work.

That is what we owe its subject.

That is what we owe the people who invite it to their tables.

Built from the working side of the screen

Table-tested, not theory-tested

RV Sawyer has spent decades running games across systems, genres, tones, and player temperaments. Tobacco Road Games grows out of that practical table experience, not from abstract advice written at a distance.

This is a working GM's bench, rules with scars, adventures with weather, monsters with motives, and tools that know the table is alive.

    Preferred Creator

    $20 per month under a 12-month commitment, or $200 annually prepaid. Preferred Creators are committed leaders of the Tobacco Road Games Creator Community. This service status does not grant operator, moderation, ownership, governance, or organic-ranking authority.

    Creator Balance must cover the full charge. Split tender is not available and balance is never spent automatically.

    Creator services are non-refundable once service begins, except for Tobacco Road Games billing mistakes or service failures.

    Close Creator Account

    Closing stops selling and pauses listings without deleting marketplace history. Existing customers keep their purchases and downloads. Your ordinary customer account, My Library, and order history remain available.

    Legitimate Creator earnings remain owed. Pending, held, disputed, reserved, or negative balances can delay completion. Annual prepaid Creator services are not refunded or credited, and existing monthly commitment obligations are not forgiven. No new renewal commitment will begin.

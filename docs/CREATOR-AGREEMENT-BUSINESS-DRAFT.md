# Tobacco Road Games Creator Agreement — Business Draft

Status: business-policy draft for counsel review; not final legal advice or production contract text.

Creators retain ownership of their work. The Creator grants Tobacco Road Games only the nonexclusive operational license needed to display, market, sell, deliver, and preserve legitimate customer access to marketplace products. No exclusivity is created.

Every acquisition requires a verified customer email, while creation of a customer account remains optional. Refunds are limited to objectively demonstrable product or transaction defects, including corrupt or wrong files, material misrepresentation, or delivery failure. Subjective dislike, perceived poor quality, changed expectations, or buyer’s remorse do not alone create refund eligibility.

When Tobacco Road Games confirms a Creator-caused defect or material misrepresentation, the product is immediately delisted and the Creator is notified. The Creator has 30 days to submit a correction. Each affected customer may choose an immediate refund or retain the entitlement while waiting. A timely corrected product is delivered through the existing entitlement after compliance review. If no acceptable correction arrives within 30 days, waiting customers are refunded as appropriate and the product remains delisted.

Refunds reverse the original sale-time Creator and Tobacco Road Games shares without recalculating historical terms. Responsibility determines actual provider costs, without markup: the Creator bears provider refund costs caused by the Creator’s defect or material misrepresentation; Tobacco Road Games bears costs caused by Tobacco Road Games.

Tobacco Road Games bears ordinary payment-fraud losses and provider dispute fees where the Creator did not materially contribute. A Creator bears attributable transaction loss and actual provider dispute fees arising from Creator fraud, unauthorized material, material misrepresentation, or similar Creator conduct. Tobacco Road Games bears losses it causes. Disputed funds remain unavailable while a dispute is open.

Creator-responsible reversals may produce a negative balance. Tobacco Road Games will not automatically charge the Creator’s stored card or debit a bank account to collect it. Future earnings first reduce the negative balance; only a positive eligible balance may be withdrawn. Tobacco Road Games may separately seek repayment of a substantial unresolved balance after selling ends.

Payouts are requested by the Creator rather than forced monthly. The normal minimum request is $10. Only one request may be pending per Creator identity. Holds, disputes, negative balances, reconciliation issues, legal restrictions, and provider limitations remain excluded. Tobacco Road Games absorbs ordinary payout/transfer fees. A failed payout preserves or restores the Creator’s funds for retry. On account closure, every remaining positive eligible balance is paid even below $10, subject to the same legitimate restrictions.

This draft preserves the existing marketplace rules concerning legal capacity, truthful information, rights and licenses, listing and inactivity rules, marketplace fees, customer entitlements, operational enforcement, and independent-contractor status. Tobacco Road Games is not the Creator’s employer, partner, agent, attorney, accountant, or tax preparer.

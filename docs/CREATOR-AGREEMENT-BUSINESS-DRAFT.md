# Tobacco Road Games Creator Agreement — Business Draft

**Status:** Business draft for counsel review. This is not final legal advice or final contract language.

**Agreement ID:** `trg-creator-marketplace-agreement`<br>
**Agreement version:** `2026-08-27`<br>
**Business-draft date:** August 30, 2026

## 1. Agreement and parties

This Creator Agreement describes the business relationship between Tobacco Road Games, the operator of the Tobacco Road Games marketplace, and the person or entity registering a Creator identity (“Creator”). Published marketplace policies provide operational detail consistent with these terms. Material changes require Tobacco Road Games approval and may require acceptance of a new version.

## 2. Who may register

Anyone legally able to enter this Agreement who satisfies the objective registration, payment-method, payout, and marketplace requirements may become a Creator. Registration is operational onboarding, not artistic approval, merit review, ideological review, or endorsement. Registration must be fully complete before product intake, drafts, uploads, pricing, publication, analytics, bundles, or listing-advertising tools become available.

## 3. Nonexclusive relationship

The relationship is expressly nonexclusive. Creator may sell the same work on Creator’s own site, through other marketplaces, directly to customers, through other publishers or distributors, and in any lawful format. Tobacco Road Games receives no exclusive distribution right. Tobacco Road Games intends to compete for Creator business by operating a useful marketplace, not by restricting where Creators sell.

## 4. Creator account and registration

Creator must provide materially accurate, current account, identity, business, address, and contact information and keep it reasonably current. Public profile information is separated from private registration and operational information. Creator is responsible for activity by people Creator authorizes to use the account.

Every Creator identity must maintain a valid Stripe-hosted payment method and complete required Stripe Connect payout onboarding. Stripe, rather than Tobacco Road Games, handles sensitive card, identity, bank, tax, and know-your-customer information. Tobacco Road Games stores only necessary provider references and limited safe status or display information. The registration hard gate remains in effect until the customer account, Creator identity, business information, current Agreement acceptance, payment method, payout setup, and identity entitlement are complete.

## 5. Ownership and limited operational license

Creator retains ownership of Creator’s products and intellectual property. Ownership does not transfer to Tobacco Road Games.

Creator grants Tobacco Road Games a limited, worldwide, nonexclusive license only as reasonably necessary to display, market, offer, sell, deliver, and preserve customer access to products; provide customer support; process transactions; and conduct technical, security, backup, archival, and recovery operations. This includes the practical ability to reproduce, format, cache, transmit, and display submitted files and promotional materials for those purposes. It does not grant ownership or an exclusive distribution right.

**Attorney review required:** Counsel must confirm the worldwide scope and duration of this limited operational license without changing its nonexclusive character or expanding it beyond marketplace operation and customer-entitlement fulfillment.

## 6. Rights, permissions, and Creator warranties

Creator represents and warrants that:

- Creator owns the submitted material or has sufficient authority to sell and distribute it.
- Distribution does not knowingly violate copyright, trademark, trade-secret, privacy, publicity, contract, or other protected rights.
- Creator has obtained required licenses, releases, consents, and permissions and will satisfy required attribution and license conditions.
- Listings materially and accurately describe what customers receive.
- Submitted material is not knowingly unlawful, fraudulent, defamatory, malicious, or infected with malware.
- Information supplied to Tobacco Road Games is materially accurate.

When a legitimate complaint or credible inconsistency arises, Tobacco Road Games may request reasonable evidence of ownership, authorization, licensing, or permission. Creator will reasonably cooperate.

## 7. Marketplace review

Marketplace review is limited to objective operational concerns including legality, seller rights, accurate representation, technical safety, file integrity, delivery, fraud, malware, and marketplace-policy compliance. Review, publication, or continued availability is not an endorsement of artistic quality, creative merit, ideology, commercial potential, or production methodology.

## 8. Marketplace fees and sale-time snapshots

For the first 30 days of a durable product identity’s first-ever public lifetime, Creator receives 90% and Tobacco Road Games receives 10% of applicable product revenue. After that launch period, a Standard Creator receives 80% and Tobacco Road Games receives 20%. While Preferred Creator status is active, Creator receives 90% and Tobacco Road Games receives 10%.

Every sale records the split and policy reason in effect at sale time. Historical sales are not recalculated when later policies, fees, or Creator status change. Editing, pausing, repricing, delisting, relisting, reactivation, or return from inactivity does not restart the original launch period. A marketplace-wide promotional split event may reduce Tobacco Road Games’ share and improve Creator’s share, but it will not reduce Creator’s share without Creator’s agreement.

## 9. Preferred Creator

Preferred Creator is a paid service tier, not an award, merit badge, or endorsement. It costs $20 per month under a 12-month commitment or $200 prepaid for one year. A missed monthly installment has a seven-calendar-day grace period during which the commitment remains in force and benefits remain provisionally active while payment is retried or cured. Continued nonpayment after day seven suspends Preferred benefits without canceling the installment obligation or commitment. Cancellation stops renewal but does not retroactively terminate the current paid or committed term. When Preferred status ends, future eligible transactions and capacity return to Standard terms; historical transactions are not recalculated.

Settled Preferred benefits include the 90/10 split, 22 active catalog slots, five active product-ad slots, no outside-vendor dashboard advertising, Creator Notices, and appropriate promotional consideration. No benefit guarantees sales or exposure.

## 10. Additional Creator identities

One primary Creator identity is included without an identity fee. Genuinely separate additional identities cost $10 per month or $100 prepaid per year. If an additional identity is also Preferred, the Preferred fee is additive. Additional identities may not be used to evade limits, multiply free benefits, manipulate promotion, or game marketplace systems.

## 11. Catalog capacity

A Standard Creator may maintain 20 active products. A Preferred Creator may maintain 22 active products. Paused, inactive, and properly delisted products do not occupy active capacity where marketplace policy so provides. When capacity is vacated, Creator may immediately use that slot for another eligible product, subject to registration, compliance, and publication requirements.

## 12. Product activity, inactivity, and relisting

A paid product must record at least one legitimate sale during each rolling 12-month period. A Free product or Pay What You Want product acquired at $0 must record at least one qualifying legitimate acquisition or download during each rolling 12-month period. A Pay What You Want product may qualify through either legitimate paid or acquisition activity.

When a product reaches the 12-month inactivity threshold, Tobacco Road Games provides a 30-day warning and grace period. Qualifying legitimate activity recorded during that 30-day period preserves the active listing. If no qualifying activity is recorded by the end of the 30-day period, Tobacco Road Games will delist the product, remove it from new sale or acquisition, remove it from advertising eligibility, free its active catalog slot, and preserve its historical records and customer entitlements. Creator may immediately use the freed slot for another eligible product.

An inactivity-delisted product must remain delisted for at least one full calendar month before relisting. Relisting requires available catalog capacity and continued marketplace compliance. Relisting does not restart the original 30-day launch period. This inactivity period is separate from the 30-day defect-repair process in Section 18.

## 13. Pay What You Want products

A Standard Creator may have no more than one active Pay What You Want listing; a Preferred Creator may have no more than two. Each counts toward active catalog capacity. Creator may set a suggested price and permit a $0 choice. A $0 acquisition bypasses Stripe payment processing but must create the required acquisition, order, and entitlement record. A positive payment uses the normal sale-time split applied to the amount actually paid.

## 14. Advertising and Ad Credits

Creator identities may use included product-ad slots or purchase Ad Credits under published marketplace terms. Creator-side purchases require a valid payment method. Advertising inventory, rotation, promotional consideration, and Ad Credits do not guarantee impressions, frequency, clicks, sales, conversions, or commercial performance. Tobacco Road Games makes no hidden performance promise.

## 15. Verified email for every acquisition

Every paid, Free, and Pay What You Want-at-$0 acquisition requires a verified email. Customer account creation remains optional. A signed-in customer uses the verified account email. A guest must verify the supplied email before checkout or acquisition completes. The verified email supports delivery, recovery, entitlement claim or later account association, support, and proportionate fraud prevention. Anonymous acquisition is not permitted.

## 16. Duplicate digital purchase prevention

When Tobacco Road Games can establish through an account, verified email, order history, or entitlement that a customer already owns the same durable digital product, the customer may not purchase it again through the ordinary digital purchase flow. Tobacco Road Games directs the customer to My Library, order recovery, or existing entitlement and download access. Repeat downloads remain available through legitimate entitlement. Duplicate purchase is prevented rather than treated as a routine refund category.

## 17. Refund eligibility

Refunds are not available merely because a customer dislikes a product, considers it poor quality, changes the customer’s mind, or experiences subjective disappointment when the listing was materially accurate.

Refunds are appropriate for objectively demonstrable problems such as a corrupt or unusable file, delivery of the wrong file, material misrepresentation, a material mismatch between the listing and delivered product, marketplace or technical failure preventing proper delivery, or another objectively demonstrable defect.

Creator service purchases, including Preferred fees, additional-identity fees, and Ad Credit packages, are non-refundable once service begins except for a genuine Tobacco Road Games billing mistake or service failure. Change of mind, non-use, voluntary inactivity, and unused benefits are not refund grounds. Approved corrections return funds to the original payment source, reverse the corrected service revenue, preserve the original purchase and audit history, and adjust an entitlement only as needed to correct the Tobacco Road Games error. Ordinary proration is unavailable.

Unused Ad Credits do not expire and remain available until redeemed. They are not cash-equivalent and are not refundable merely for non-use. A redeemed credit starts its 30-day slot; ad removal, creative swaps, or failure to receive impressions, clicks, or sales do not create refund rights or guaranteed advertising results.

## 18. Creator-caused defect and 30-day repair process

Once Tobacco Road Games confirms a Creator-caused defect or material misrepresentation, Tobacco Road Games will immediately delist the product and notify Creator. Creator has 30 days to submit an acceptable correction. Each affected customer may choose an immediate refund or wait for the repaired product.

If a waiting customer’s product is acceptably corrected within 30 days, the corrected file is delivered through the existing entitlement after ordinary compliance review. If no acceptable correction arrives within 30 days, each affected customer who elected to wait will be refunded, subject only to an actual legal or payment-provider impossibility, and the product remains delisted. The product remains delisted until the correction satisfies ordinary marketplace review.

## 19. Refund accounting and provider costs

A refund reverses the Creator share and Tobacco Road Games share recorded for the refunded amount at the original sale. The original transaction, split snapshot, reversal, and audit history remain preserved. Current policy is not used to recalculate a historical sale.

Creator bears the actual provider refund-related cost attributable to a Creator-caused defect or material misrepresentation. Tobacco Road Games bears the actual provider cost attributable to a Tobacco Road Games-caused refund. No markup or administrative penalty is added.

## 20. Chargebacks and payment disputes

A chargeback or payment dispute is initiated through a customer’s bank, card issuer, Stripe, or another provider. Disputed funds are not withdrawable while open, and transaction and dispute history are preserved.

Tobacco Road Games absorbs the attributable loss and ordinary provider dispute fee for ordinary payment fraud when Creator did not materially contribute. Creator bears the attributable transaction loss and actual provider fee for Creator fraud, unauthorized material, material misrepresentation, or similar Creator-side conduct. Tobacco Road Games bears a Tobacco Road Games-caused loss and fee. Provider fees allocated to Creator have no markup.

## 21. Fraud response

Tobacco Road Games may suspend or ban a reasonably confirmed fraudulent customer account, block a confirmed fraudulent verified email from future transactions, and retain internal records reasonably necessary for marketplace protection. IP address, network, session, device, and payment-provider signals may support review, temporary blocks, rate limits, or heightened scrutiny, but an IP address alone is not a permanent identity. Authorized operators must be able to reverse a false positive.

## 22. Negative Creator balances

Creator-responsible refunds, disputes, chargebacks, or legitimate adjustments may create a negative balance. Tobacco Road Games will not automatically charge Creator’s stored card or debit Creator’s bank account to collect it. Future eligible earnings first reduce the negative balance; only earnings above zero may become withdrawable. Withdrawals are unavailable while negative. If Creator stops selling and an unresolved negative balance remains, Tobacco Road Games retains the right to seek repayment under final counsel-approved collection terms.

**Attorney review required:** Counsel must define lawful notice, collection, dispute, and recovery procedures for an unresolved negative balance without adding an undisclosed monetary threshold or authorizing automatic card or bank debits.

## 23. Creator-requested payouts

Payouts are requested by Creator and are not forced monthly. The ordinary minimum withdrawal is $10. A positive eligible balance below $10 remains in the account. At $10 or more, Creator may request withdrawal at Creator’s discretion. Only one payout request may be pending per Creator identity.

Tobacco Road Games absorbs ordinary Stripe payout or transfer fees. A failed payout does not forfeit Creator’s money; the eligible balance is preserved or restored for retry. Disputed or held funds, negative balances, reconciliation exceptions, legal restrictions, and provider limitations are not withdrawable.

## 24. Account-closure payout

When a Creator account closes voluntarily or is closed by Tobacco Road Games, Tobacco Road Games will pay the entire remaining positive eligible balance even below $10. This is subject only to legitimate disputes, holds, negative balances, legal restrictions, and provider limitations. Tobacco Road Games will not retain a small positive eligible balance merely because it is below the ordinary minimum.

## 25. Business records and taxes

Creator is responsible for Creator’s accounting, taxes, filings, and professional advice. Tobacco Road Games provides business records including annual gross sales, Tobacco Road Games fees, refunds and chargebacks, net payouts, monthly statements, and year-to-date totals. These are business records, not tax preparation. Stripe or another provider handles its own identity, know-your-customer, tax, payout, and reporting responsibilities.

## 26. Six-month Creator account audits

Each Creator identity is subject to an operational audit on a six-month cycle. Audits address objective matters such as valid contact information, current Agreement acceptance, payment and payout readiness, entitlement accuracy, billing status, unresolved fraud or payment issues, and misuse of account privileges. They are not artistic, ideological, or merit review. Correctable problems receive a reasonable cure opportunity unless immediate restriction is necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, payment risk, or protection of customers or third parties. Restrictions do not erase historical sales, customer entitlements, financial records, or Creator data.

## 27. Customer access to prior purchases

When a product is paused, delisted, inactive, withdrawn, or unavailable to new buyers, legitimate prior purchasers may retain access where technically and legally possible. Creator may not withdraw a product solely to deprive prior purchasers. Creator authorizes Tobacco Road Games to retain necessary delivery copies and entitlement records for continuing fulfillment, support, security, and legal compliance.

**Attorney review required:** Counsel must finalize the legal mechanism and survival period for entitlement preservation after withdrawal or termination while retaining the settled business intent that legitimate prior access continues where technically and legally possible.

## 28. Intellectual-property complaints

Tobacco Road Games may investigate a credible ownership, licensing, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights complaint and temporarily restrict a product. Creator must reasonably cooperate and provide available evidence. The final formal notice, counter-notice, repeat-infringer, and complaint procedure must be reviewed and completed by counsel before launch.

## 29. Indemnification — attorney-review placeholder

**Attorney review required.** The business intent is that Creator should be responsible for third-party claims and attributable losses arising from Creator’s breach concerning ownership, licensing, authorization, protected rights, fraud, unlawful content, or material misrepresentation. The final clause must include appropriate procedures, control of defense, notice, cooperation, and proportionality. It must not shift to Creator liability for conduct or loss actually caused by Tobacco Road Games.

## 30. Suspension and termination

Tobacco Road Games may suspend or restrict a product or account for fraud, unlawful activity, malware or security threats, rights violations, unresolved payment risk, serious or repeated policy violations, legal process, or abuse of marketplace systems. Where practical and safe, Tobacco Road Games may provide notice and an opportunity to cure. Immediate action is permitted when reasonably necessary to protect customers, the marketplace, payment systems, third parties, legal compliance, or security.

## 31. Effect of termination

Creator retains ownership after termination. The operational license ends except as reasonably necessary to preserve legitimate customer access, maintain financial and accounting records, resolve refunds, disputes, or chargebacks, satisfy legal obligations, prevent fraud, and preserve required audit, security, backup, or archive records. Terms that logically must continue may survive, subject to counsel-approved final language.

**Attorney review required:** Counsel must define the survival and duration of the limited post-termination operational license and record-retention duties without transferring ownership or creating a broader continuing distribution right.

## 32. Independent marketplace seller; no other relationship

Creator is an independent marketplace seller. This Agreement creates no employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, fiduciary relationship, franchise, or authority for either party to bind the other. Tobacco Road Games is not Creator’s attorney, accountant, tax preparer, business manager, or financial adviser.

## 33. Prospective policy and agreement changes

Future marketplace policy, fee, and technical changes apply prospectively. A material Agreement change may require acceptance of a new version. Historical sale terms and immutable sale-time snapshots remain historical and are not retroactively recalculated. Tobacco Road Games will not retroactively change the marketplace percentage applied to a completed sale.

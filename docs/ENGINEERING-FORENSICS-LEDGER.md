- **Status:** Stopped before implementation at an unresolved financial policy boundary. `docs/MARKETPLACE-PROVIDER-FINANCE-PASS-8.md` conditions Stripe Connect transfer adoption on commercial/legal validation that has not been recorded. The existing Connect helper remains test-key/non-production only. TRG checkout does not set `transfer_group` or `source_transaction`, so new code cannot assume an order-to-transfer provider link exists.
- **Stripe model:** Stripe-maintained `connect-recommend` and `stripe-best-practices` agent skills were read directly. Separate charges and transfers move funds from TRG's platform Stripe balance to a Creator's connected Stripe balance. This is distinct from Stripe's subsequent payout of connected-account funds to the Creator's bank. Under the documented separate-charge flow, the platform bears charge fees, refunds and chargebacks; transfer reversals can depend on connected-account balance/reserves. Official guidance: [Connect separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers), [transfer API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/transfers/create), [transfer reversals](https://docs.stripe.com/api/transfer_reversals), and [Express Dashboard](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/integrate-express-dashboard).
- **Ambiguous-response requirement:** Stripe may prune API v1 idempotency keys after at least 24 hours. A future executor must persist a stable transfer group/reference, reconcile through Stripe's transfer listing by `transfer_group`, and refuse a blind retry when the result remains uncertain after the idempotency window. See [idempotent requests](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests) and [list transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/api/transfers/list).
- **Owner decisions required:** Confirm Connect charge/transfer configuration and loss liability; payer of transfer/charge fees, refunds, disputes and negative connected balances; Creator country/currency and tax scope; funding and reconciliation policy tying the existing Creator liability ledger to Stripe's available platform balance. After those decisions, specify isolated transfer, duplicate, timeout/reconciliation, refund and reversal tests.
- **Changes:** No transfer path, login-link action, migration, or C2A regression was added. Existing payout eligibility, reserves, holds, reservation, batch, manual record and reconciliation mechanisms remain unchanged.
- **Baseline verification:** `npm run verify:launch` passed on 2026-11-18 with 67 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped, and 1 remote-only staging check blocked. No live or test-mode Stripe resource was contacted.

About This Book

About This Book

Sirrocans presents a giant-blooded desert people shaped by black-stone wastes, buried ruins, sacred tribal songs, and survival under an unforgiving sky. The supplement combines player-facing racial traits with cultural grounding, naming traditions, roleplaying guidance, and Sirrocan equipment so the ancestry feels playable at the table instead of sketched in from a distance.

What's Inside

What's Inside

  • 5E-compatible character race for giant-blooded desert nomads
  • Six Giant Ancestry options tied to Cloud, Fire, Frost, Hill, Stone, and Storm lineages
  • Desert-Born, Powerful Build, Natural Athlete, and Wastewise Craft racial traits
  • Sirrocan names, tribes, ideals, bonds, flaws, and roleplaying guidance
  • Sirrocan Buckler and Sirrocan Shortspear cultural equipment

About This Book

About This Book

Agency: Share the Wheel or Crash the Game is a system-neutral tabletop roleplaying supplement about player choice, Game Master authority, and the shared responsibility that makes a campaign work. It cuts through the usual arguments over “agency” and gets to the table-level truth: players control their characters, the Game Master controls the world, and the game becomes meaningful when choices have honest consequences. Inside, RV Sawyer examines what agency is, what it is not, and how it breaks when either side tries to seize too much control. The supplement covers divided authority, meaningful choice, consequence, honest failure, spotlight balance, information, pacing, and the social agreement that keeps play from becoming either a guided tour through the GM’s private novel or a wish engine where nothing pushes back. Written for both Game Masters and players, Agency is practical, direct, and built from real table experience rather than theory floating around in a wizard jar. It is for groups that want campaigns shaped by decisions, worlds that answer player action, and games where failure, risk, and consequence make victory matter. Share the wheel. Let choices bite. Then hold on when the road turns.

What's Inside

  • Inside you'll find:

  • System-neutral advice usable in virtually any tabletop RPG.
